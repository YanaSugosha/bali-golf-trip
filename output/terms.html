Booking and confirmation

We will reconfirm this booking one day prior to the scheduled date of play. Please be informed that if no confirmation is received by that time, the reservation will be automatically cancelled without further.

NOTED
Please come early at least 15 minutes before your tee time

Terms and Conditions:

  1. Any cancellations received by the club on the day of play will be charged at 100%.
  2. The above golf rates are inclusive of green fee, sharing cart, caddy, player’s insurance, 2 bottles mineral water and government tax 11%.
  3. Caddy tipping is not included (standard at IDR 250.000,-/caddie)
  4. No Kids allowed on the course below 8 Years Old

Regulations:

  1. To ensure the operational running well, please be on time and group of less than 4 Ball Players will be mandatory merged with other players.(Special at New kuta Golf)
  2. Sharing a cart is mandatory.
  3. Each player should have his or her set of clubs, sharing of clubs is not permitted.
  4. Only food & beverage purchased from the club may be consumed on the course or in the clubhouse. Otherwise the corkage fee will be charged accordingly.
  5. Proper golf attire (Polo golf shirt, golf short or trouser) and soft spiked golf shoes with socks are required. No jeans are permitted
