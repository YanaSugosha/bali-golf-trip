The colours are named for the course

Fairway, forest, birdie, flag. The palette borrows the language of the game rather than numbering itself primary and secondary, so anyone briefing a change can say which green they mean. Every colour on the website resolves to one of the twelve values below.

Course colours

Six values carry the brand

Slate is the ink and the dark ground. Fairway is every action; forest is that action pressed. Birdie only appears on dark. Flag is reserved for a saving or an alert — never decoration. Sunrise is the logo’s yellow: the active dot in every carousel, and a price that is not set yet.

Sunrise, in use

Yellow is a ground, not a colour of type

Against white it scores 1.34:1 and all but disappears. It reads only with slate behind or in front of it — 12.96:1 — so on the site it is always a ground with slate ink on top.
